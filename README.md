# Strategic Planning, Data Exploration, Predictive Modeling and Optimization in Logistics

## Delhivery Logistics Data Analysis – Indian E-Commerce Market

### Project Overview

This project is a **multi-week Data Analytics and Data Science case study focused on the Indian logistics and e-commerce industry**, using **Delhivery Limited** as the case-study company.

The project demonstrates how logistics data can be transformed into useful business insights through a progressive workflow:

**Strategic Planning → Data Exploration → Advanced Analytics → Predictive Modeling → Optimization → Business Recommendations**

The objective is to understand and analyze logistics operations such as delivery performance, delivery delays, shipment characteristics, operational workload, transportation factors, cost-related drivers, and potential logistics risks.

> **Academic Case Study:** This project does not use or claim access to Delhivery's confidential or proprietary internal operational data. Where real operational data is unavailable, public, anonymized, or simulated datasets are used for educational analysis. Results should not be interpreted as actual Delhivery performance.

---

# Project Objectives

The overall objectives of this project are to:

- Analyze logistics and delivery performance in the Indian market.
- Understand major factors affecting delivery time.
- Identify important logistics KPIs.
- Explore shipment, route, regional, and operational patterns.
- Analyze potential causes of delivery delays.
- Study freight and transportation-related factors.
- Identify potential RTO and operational-risk factors.
- Build predictive models for delivery-time estimation.
- Evaluate and compare different machine-learning models.
- Identify important features influencing delivery time.
- Develop logistics optimization strategies.
- Support data-driven resource allocation.
- Convert analytical findings into actionable business recommendations.

# Company Case Study

## Delhivery Limited

Delhivery is an Indian integrated logistics services provider operating across a large logistics network.

Its logistics-related services include areas such as:

- Express parcel delivery
- Freight transportation
- Warehousing
- Supply-chain solutions
- Reverse logistics
- Cross-border logistics

Delhivery is used as the case-study organization because its business is closely related to e-commerce logistics, shipment movement, last-mile delivery, warehousing, tracking, and large-scale logistics operations.

---

# Business Problem

The central business question is:

> **How can logistics data and data science techniques be used to improve delivery reliability, control logistics costs, identify operational risks, and allocate logistics resources more effectively?**

The project investigates questions such as:

- Which regions experience higher delivery delays?
- What factors influence delivery time?
- How do traffic and weather conditions affect delivery operations?
- How does hub workload affect delivery performance?
- How does shipment distance affect delivery time?
- Which shipment characteristics are associated with longer delivery times?
- Can delivery time be predicted before shipment completion?
- Which variables are most important for prediction?
- How can high-risk shipments be identified?
- How can logistics resources be allocated more effectively?
